## What I learned
The more experience someone has with a specific task, the less I should control them. New to the task: clear steps and frequent check-ins. Experienced: agree on the goal, then step back.

## How it helps me
I stop micromanaging experienced people, and I stop leaving new people alone too early.

## When to use it
- Giving a new developer their first client integration
- A senior project manager feels I check on them too often
